Undercover Imposter

Pass & Play + Online Multiplayer Game

How to playQuick guide +
🕵️

1. Get your secret word
Pass the phone around. Each player privately checks their word. One or more players may be imposters.

💬

2. Give a clue
Take turns giving a short clue without saying the actual word.

🗳️

3. Discuss and vote
Find who has a different word—or is bluffing. Vote for the suspected imposter.

🏆

4. Reveal and score
Catch all imposters to win as citizens. If an imposter survives, the imposters win.
