Privacy Policy

Undercover Imposter · Effective date: 25 October 2026

This policy explains how the Undercover Imposter mobile game handles information when you use the app and its online features.

1. Information the game may process

When you use online features, the app may create an anonymous guest account and process a guest identifier, the player name you choose, room codes, game-session information and gameplay statistics needed to operate online rooms and profiles. Some game settings and progress may be stored locally on your device.

2. How information is used

Information is used to provide game functionality, create and join online rooms, maintain player profiles and statistics, troubleshoot problems and protect the service from misuse.

3. Service providers

Online features use Supabase infrastructure for authentication and backend services. The app may use InMobi or other advertising partners to serve ads when advertising is enabled. Those providers may process device, network, advertising and ad-interaction information under their own privacy terms.

  • Supabase Privacy Policy
  • InMobi Privacy Policy

4. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by the service providers described above to operate the game, or disclosed when required by law or to protect users and the service.

5. Children

Undercover Imposter is a general-audience party game and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children under the age required by applicable law. Parents or guardians who believe a child has provided personal information can contact the developer using the support link below.

6. Data retention and choices

Online game data is retained as needed to provide the service and maintain game records. You can avoid online features and use local gameplay where available. For a request about access or deletion of online data, contact the developer and include your player name and relevant room details; do not send passwords or sensitive identity documents.

7. Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the app’s features change. The effective date above indicates the latest revision.
